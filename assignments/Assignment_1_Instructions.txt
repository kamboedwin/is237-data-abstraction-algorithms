IS 237 — Assignment 1
Problem-Solving and Algorithm Design Case Study

Purpose
Demonstrate that you can move from a real problem to a clear algorithmic solution before writing programme code.

Task
Choose ONE everyday, university, community, data-science or AI-related problem that can be solved by a step-by-step computational procedure.

Your report must include:
1. Problem statement: what is the problem and why does it matter?
2. Inputs and outputs.
3. Constraints and assumptions.
4. Decomposition into smaller subproblems.
5. Proposed problem-solving strategy and why it fits.
6. A flowchart OR structured pseudocode for the main algorithm.
7. A manual trace using at least one example input.
8. At least four test cases: typical, boundary, special, and (where meaningful) invalid.
9. A short correctness explanation: why should the procedure produce the required result?
10. A first efficiency discussion: what is the input size n, and does the work appear constant, linear, quadratic, or another growth pattern?
11. One possible improvement or alternative approach.

Expected length
3–5 pages excluding cover page and references/appendices.

Important
The emphasis is on reasoning, not on programming syntax. You may include code only after your algorithm has been clearly explained.
